Intro chemistry · Chemical reactions

How reactions happen

A reaction rearranges atoms. It does not create them or destroy them. The connections change; the atom count stays the same.

Atoms in, atoms out

In a chemical reaction, bonds break and new bonds form. The atoms themselves stay the same atoms. That idea is called conservation of atoms (and of mass, when you weigh everything).

A chemical equation is atom bookkeeping. The left side lists the reactants. The right side lists the products. The same kinds of atoms must appear on both sides, in the same numbers.

Subscripts are part of a formula. They tell you how many atoms are already joined in one molecule. You cannot change a subscript to balance an equation, because that would invent a different substance. Coefficients sit in front of a formula. They tell you how many of that molecule you need. Coefficients are the dials you turn to balance.

01

Combination · water from H₂ and O₂

Same atoms, new connections

2 H₂ + O₂ → 2 H₂O

Two hydrogen molecules and one oxygen molecule rearrange into two water molecules. Count the atoms: four H and two O on both sides.

This is a combination (synthesis) reaction: smaller molecules join into a larger product. No atom is invented or thrown away.

02

Combustion · methane

Burning is a reaction

CH₄ + 2 O₂ → CO₂ + 2 H₂O

Methane burns in oxygen to make carbon dioxide and water. One carbon, four hydrogens, and four oxygens sit on each side of the arrow.

Combustion joins a fuel with oxygen and releases energy as heat and light. The atoms still balance.

C H O

Measured C–H 1.087 Å, C=O 1.162 Å, O=O 1.2075 Å, O–H 0.958 Å (NIST CCCBDB). Shapes from PubChem 3D.

03

Decomposition · hydrogen peroxide

One substance splits

2 H₂O₂ → 2 H₂O + O₂

Hydrogen peroxide breaks into water and oxygen gas. Four hydrogens and four oxygens appear on both sides.

A decomposition reaction starts with one kind of compound and ends with simpler substances. The O–O bond in peroxide is the weak link that breaks first in many real pathways; the stage only shows the balanced start and finish, not every intermediate.

H O

Measured H₂O₂: O–H 0.950 Å, O–O 1.475 Å, ∠HOO 94.8° (NIST CCCBDB). PubChem 3D shape on stage.

Type · combination

A + B → AB

Two or more substances join into one product. Making water from H₂ and O₂ is the classroom example above.

Type · decomposition

AB → A + B

One compound splits into simpler pieces. Peroxide → water + oxygen is the example above.

Type · combustion

Fuel + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O

A fuel reacts with oxygen. Complete combustion of a hydrocarbon makes carbon dioxide and water, as with methane.

In the real world

  • HVAC techniciansNatural gas furnaces burn methane with oxygen from air. HVAC technicians install and service those burners so combustion stays complete and safe.Combustion · CH₄ + O₂
  • Auto techniciansGasoline engines burn hydrocarbon fuel with oxygen. Auto technicians diagnose incomplete combustion when exhaust sensors and catalytic converters show trouble.Combustion · fuel + O₂
  • FirefightersFire needs fuel, oxygen, and heat. Firefighters remove one side of that triangle with water, foam, or smothering agents, and they teach that combustion is a chemical reaction, not just “flame.”Combustion triangle
  • Wastewater operatorsHydrogen peroxide is used in some treatment steps to oxidize contaminants. Operators dose and monitor peroxide carefully because it decomposes to water and oxygen.Decomposition · H₂O₂

For the classroom

Check yourself

Does a chemical reaction create new atoms?

No. It rearranges the atoms that are already there. The same atoms appear on both sides of a balanced equation.

What is the difference between a subscript and a coefficient?

A subscript is part of the formula (the ₂ in H₂O). Changing it makes a different substance. A coefficient sits in front (the 2 in 2 H₂O) and tells how many molecules you need. You balance by changing coefficients only.

Is CH₄ + O₂ → CO₂ + H₂O balanced?

No. The left side has four H and two O; the right has two H and three O. The balanced equation is CH₄ + 2 O₂ → CO₂ + 2 H₂O.

Name the reaction type for 2 H₂O₂ → 2 H₂O + O₂.

Decomposition. One compound (hydrogen peroxide) splits into simpler products (water and oxygen).

Why is 2 H₂ + O₂ → 2 H₂O preferred over H₂ + ½ O₂ → H₂O?

Both conserve atoms, but classroom equations usually use the smallest whole-number coefficients, so you do not need a half molecule of O₂.

Where the numbers come from

  • 3D shapes of O₂, H₂O, CH₄, CO₂, and H₂O₂: PubChem 3D conformers (CID 977, 962, 297, 280, 784). H₂ has no PubChem 3D conformer, so it is drawn at its measured H–H length.
  • Measured bond lengths and angles: NIST Computational Chemistry Comparison and Benchmark Database (CCCBDB), experimental data.
  • Balanced equations follow atom conservation for the stated stoichiometric reactions; coefficients are the smallest whole numbers.
